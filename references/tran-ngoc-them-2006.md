# Trần Ngọc Thêm — hệ thống liên kết văn bản tiếng Việt

Source: Trần Ngọc Thêm, *Hệ thống liên kết văn bản tiếng Việt*, Hà Nội: NXB Giáo
dục. First edition 1985; the scanned copy used here is the third edition (2006,
`Tái bản lần thứ ba`). Section numbers (§) follow the book's `Mục lục`.

The book is not a translation of Halliday & Hasan. It builds a Vietnamese system
around the **phát ngôn** (utterance) and around the distinction between **liên
kết hình thức** (formal cohesion) and **liên kết nội dung** (content cohesion).
Do not map its devices one-to-one onto the English five; see
`en-vi-mapping.md`.

## 1. Two faces of cohesion (§5)

- **Liên kết nội dung** — the utterances cohere in topic and in logic.
- **Liên kết hình thức** — formal devices realize that content cohesion.
- A text needs both; formal links alone can produce a chain of sentences that is
  still not a text (§5.2). This is the Vietnamese counterpart of Halliday &
  Hasan's point that cohesion is not sufficient for coherence.
- **Liên kết chủ đề** (topic) and **liên kết logic** (logic) are the two content
  faces (§6–§7).
- **Liên kết tiếp giáp** (adjacent) vs **liên kết bắc cầu** (bridging over
  intervening utterances); **liên kết đơn** (one relation) vs **liên kết phức**
  (several simultaneous relations) (§8). §9 is the later classification of
  devices by utterance type.

## 2. The unit: phát ngôn (§10–§24)

The book replaces the sentence with the **phát ngôn** and classifies it by
semantic and structural completeness:

| Type | Structure | Meaning | Typical linkage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Câu tự nghĩa** (CTN) | complete | self-contained | all common devices; can stand alone |
| **Câu hợp nghĩa** (CHN) | complete | semantically incomplete; needs another utterance | zero linkage (*khiếm diện*), pro-forms, weak ellipsis, loose conjunction |
| **Ngữ trực thuộc** (NTT) | incomplete | incomplete | strong ellipsis, tight conjunction |

This classification matters for annotation: a device that is normal for a CHN
(e.g. a zero subject) is not automatically an ellipsis in a CTN.

## 3. Present vs absent linkage (§23)

- **Liên kết hiện diện** — the linking elements are on the surface.
- **Liên kết khiếm diện** — the link is to something absent from the surface
  (reality, situation, or an omitted element). It is the *base* relation, not a
  defect (§23.2).
- In a present link, the utterance that carries the signal is the **kết ngôn**;
  the more independent one is the **chủ ngôn**; the linking element in the
  kết ngôn is the **kết tố**, in the chủ ngôn the **chủ tố** (§23.4). Direction:
  **hồi quy** (retrospective) or **dự báo** (prospective).

## 4. Devices common to all three utterance types (§25–§33)

1. **Phép lặp** (§26–§29): lexical repetition, grammatical repetition,
   phonetic repetition.
2. **Phép đối** (§30): parallelism / antithesis.
3. **Phép thế đẳng nghĩa** (§31): substitution by a synonymous expression.
4. **Phép liên tưởng** (§32): associative linkage between semantically related
   items.
5. **Phép tuyến tính** (§33): linkage by linear order alone.

## 5. Devices for câu hợp nghĩa (§34–§39)

- **Phép thế đại từ** (§35–§36): pronouns and pro-forms, with three subtypes —
  **khiếm diện** (zero pronoun), **dự báo** (prospective/cataphoric), **hồi quy**
  (retrospective/anaphoric).
- **Phép tỉnh lược yếu / liên kết** (§37): weak ellipsis.
- **Phép nối lỏng / liên kết** (§38): loose conjunction.

## 6. Devices for ngữ trực thuộc (§40–§44)

- **Phép tỉnh lược mạnh** (§41–§42): strong ellipsis (the dependent utterance
  cannot stand alone).
- **Phép nối chặt** (§43): tight conjunction.

## 7. Content cohesion (§45–§51)

- The concept extends below and above the utterance, to morphemes and to
  paragraphs (§46), and is claimed to be universal (§47).
- **Liên kết chủ đề** (§49–§51): topic cohesion, with **song song** (parallel)
  and **móc xích** (chain) patterns, plus degrees of topic cohesion and topic
  complexity.
- Topic cohesion is analysed separately from formal devices; it is not a tie in
  the Appendix sense.

## 8. Terms that are easy to confuse

| Term | What it is | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| **thế đại từ khiếm diện** | zero pronoun: a referent is tracked with no overt form | **tỉnh lược**: omission of wording recoverable from co-text |
| **phép thế đẳng nghĩa** | replacement by a synonymous expression | Halliday's grammatical **substitution** (*one/do/so*) |
| **phép liên tưởng** | associative semantic link | simple topic continuity |
| **phép tuyến tính** | order-based linkage | a lexical or grammatical tie |
| **phép đối** | parallelism/antithesis | reference |
| **liên kết khiếm diện** | linkage to what is absent from the surface | a missing tie / an error |

## 9. Consequences for the Appendix

1. A Vietnamese **zero subject** may be analysed as *thế đại từ khiếm diện*
   (reference-like) or as *tỉnh lược* (ellipsis). This skill codes a zero slot as
   **E** for the Appendix; record the *khiếm diện* reading in §2/§4 of the
   analysis and do not double-code it as R.
2. **Repetition of a full noun** is a normal Vietnamese cohesive device
   (*phép lặp từ vựng*), not a failure to use a pronoun. Code it **L**, and note
   the EN→VI shift when the English side used R.
3. **Phép thế đẳng nghĩa** and **phép liên tưởng** are lexical: code **L**, not
   **S**.
4. **Phép nối** is a semantic relation; a zero connector can still be a C tie if
   the relation is recoverable, and a connector word alone does not create one.
5. **Phép tuyến tính** and **phép đối** are not ties by themselves; only an
   identifiable lexical pair (L) is recorded.
